EMG BAND - FINAL PRINT SET AND BUILD STEPS

FILES (print these)
  box.stl            upper-arm cuff box, PETG, 4 walls. Print with the curved outer surface up on supports, or on an end wall.
  lid.stl            curved lid with rounded rim, PETG, same orientation.
  plate.stl          sew-on mounting plate, PETG, concave side up on supports.
  electrode_puck.stl x2, PETG, flat.       shim.stl x4, PETG, flat (1 mm spacers behind the pucks)
  imu_puck.stl       x1, PETG, flat.        imu_lid.stl x1, PETG, flat.
  band.scad          source; measured: charger 19x6.5x7, IMU 24x14.5. Guessed: electrode board 38x22x4 (measure when sensors arrive), arm R44.

WHAT GOES WHERE
  In the box:  Pico W on a 25x60 strip of perfboard (4 M2 screws into the tilted standoffs), EEMB 1200 mAh battery on a strip of
               foam in the tilted bay, SunFounder charger module standing in its pocket (pins down into the floor slot), slide switch
               in the CHEST-facing side wall near the elbow end (front=1 in band.scad; set -1 for the other arm), 2N2222 + 1k + 1N4148 motor driver on the strip, 2x 100k battery divider on the strip.
  In the lid:  coin motor in the ring cradle on the lid underside over the Pico side.
  In the lid:  Qi receiver board in the small frame over the charger corner, its coil taped flat on the lid underside with the ferrite sheet facing the Pico, red lead -> 1N5817 -> Pico VBUS, black -> GND; 6 mm tactile button through the hole on the CHEST-side edge, 3 mm RGB LED dome through the
               hole with 3x 220 ohm on the strip. Later: Qi receiver coil against the lid underside with its ferrite sheet toward the Pico.
  Under the box: plate with 4x M2x4 heat-set inserts in its bosses, sewn to the sleeve through the eyelets. Box screws down with 4x M2x8.
  On the forearm loop (1.5 in knit elastic sewn into a ring): 2 electrode pucks each holding a SEN0240 electrode board dots-down
               (signal board in the same puck if its cable is short), 1 IMU puck holding the GY-BNO08X on the slotted bosses.
  Between:     one 6-wire trunk (3V3, GND, sig1, sig2, SDA, SCL) in 1/4 in braided sleeving, heat-shrink collars, JST-PH both ends;
               plugs into the IMU puck (acting as hub) below and the box's elbow-end exit above; lazy S across the elbow.

PICO PINS
  GP26 sensor 1 (flexor)   GP27 sensor 2 (extensor)   GP28 battery divider midpoint
  GP4 SDA / GP5 SCL  -> BNO08X (PS0, PS1, AD0 to GND; RST, BOOT to 3V3)
  GP14 button to GND (pull-up)   GP15 -> 1k -> 2N2222 base; motor 3V3 to collector, 1N4148 across motor
  GP16/17/18 -> 220 ohm -> RGB LED
  Charger module: VBUS, VSYS, GND pins of the Pico.  Slide switch: between charger OUT and Pico VSYS (off = band dead, still charges).

BUILD ORDER
  1. Print pucks, shims, IMU lid, plate first (no waiting). Print box + lid once the sensors are measured (or now with the guess).
  2. Heat-set 4 M2 inserts into the plate bosses. Score/snap a 4x6 perfboard to 25x60; solder Pico on male pins; JST sockets at both ends.
  3. Wire the strip: motor driver, divider, LED resistors, three wires to the charger module, switch on the VSYS lead.
  4. Bench test on the desk with the electrodes stuck to your own arm: Wi-Fi up, packets flowing, button/LED/motor working.
  5. Conformal-coat the strip and charger (nail polish or spray), Kapton under the Pico, foam under the battery.
  6. Assemble the box: battery, charger, strip, switch; lid with motor/button/LED; screw to the plate.
  7. Sew: plate to the sleeve's upper section, 4 slots on the forearm section for the loop, cable loops up the outside.
  8. Loop: sew elastic into a ring sized to the forearm, thread pucks on, sensors in, IMU in, plug the trunk.
  9. Fit on your arm, then Kyle's: mark the spots, add shims until the pads press firmly, tune thresholds on the arcade.
